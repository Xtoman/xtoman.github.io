Toys That Invented Me

The series "Toys That Invented Me" shows Soviet childhood objects as artificial intelligence might see them — white background, centered composition, isolated objects turned into neutral data for recognition: shape, material, color, label. But behind this optic lie the memories of a generation that played with these toys, learned from these blocks, counted on these abacuses, and then built the technologies that made AI possible. Each frame is an imagined algorithmic gaze on a past it never had, but without which it could not exist.

2026 Moscow, Russia
